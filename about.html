About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Eastern Kentucky University with a focus on game development. I want to build games — not just play them — and I've been working toward that by learning every layer of the process: programming, 3D modeling, animation, and game engine work.

Background

I'm from Lexington, KY, and I've been into games my whole life. At some point playing them wasn't enough — I wanted to understand how they were made. That curiosity led me to CS, which led me to Unity, which led me to realizing I could build both sides: the code and the art. Outside of development, I stay active through exercise and keep sharp by playing the kinds of games I want to make one day.
